Core Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 2026-10-08

The Vibe Terminal developer (jhl-labs, “we”) handles information as described below. This policy applies to the free Vibe Terminal Core edition, listed on Google Play as Vibe Terminal: SSH 터미널, Android package com.euno.vibe_terminal. It does not automatically apply to a separate Pro product.

Basic terminal work takes place between your device and the server you choose.

Core does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging or advertising SDKs. Optional AI, GitHub sync and community features, and update checks communicate with external services.

1. Information stored on your device

To provide SSH connections, restore work and deliver features you choose, the app may process the following information locally:

  • Host labels, addresses, ports, usernames, connection settings and trusted host key fingerprints
  • SSH passwords, private keys, passphrases, and AI and GitHub authentication tokens
  • Terminal screens, session history, commands, output, working paths, snippets, notes and settings
  • AI conversations, prompts, submitted text context, and provider, model and endpoint settings

Authentication secrets use the operating system's secure storage. Other settings and work data use local app storage; this does not mean all local data is separately encrypted. Core does not require an account operated by the developer.

3. Permissions and security

Network access supports SSH and selected services. Android foreground service, notification and wake lock functions maintain SSH connections you start and display their status. File and clipboard functions support actions you request, such as imports, exports and pasting.

The app uses encrypted SSH transport, host key checks and operating system secure storage. The Android distribution configuration restricts system backups and automatic device-to-device extraction of app data. It does not control exported files, remote backups or data already sent to servers. Check transport security for endpoints you configure.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Local hosts, keys, snippets, notes, conversations and records may remain until you delete them or clear app data, or according to the app's history settings.
  • Deleting one item may leave shared credentials or keys used by other hosts, or separate work history. Remove unneeded credentials, keys, conversations and records in their respective screens.
  • On Android, clear the app's storage in system settings or uninstall the app to remove its dedicated local data.
  • Uninstalling does not delete GitHub repositories, Gists, posts, external AI or SSH server data, or exported files. Delete these separately at the service or storage location; previous content may remain in Git history or provider backups.

We cannot remotely access, restore or delete SSH credentials or work records stored only on your device.

5. Support information and your rights

Contact bkperio@gmail.com for privacy inquiries or requests to access, correct, delete or stop processing support information we hold. Contact owner: Vibe Terminal developer (jhl-labs).

When you email us, we use your email address, message and attachments you choose to send to respond and troubleshoot. Messages are received through Gmail and also processed by Google's email service. Do not send passwords, private keys, tokens or unredacted personal logs. We delete support information when it is no longer needed for its purpose, except where a legal retention obligation applies.

6. Website and video

GitHub Pages hosts this website and GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses for service delivery and security. We add no advertising or visitor analytics scripts. Language and theme preferences on the introduction page are saved in browser local storage, which you can clear by deleting site data.

The Core demonstration video is served by vcoder.kr. Playing it may send your IP address, browser information and media requests to that server. Video does not autoplay. VCoder privacy policy

7. Children

The app is a terminal tool for developers, operators and similar users. It is not designed for children under 14 and we do not intentionally collect their personal information. Contact us with any concerns.

8. Changes

We update this page and its revision date when features or data practices change. Material changes will be announced through the introduction site or release information before they take effect.
